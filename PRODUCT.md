# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Talent without access:** actors and models with no agent, no credits and no connections. They come to get seen: read how access works, find open casting calls, and submit through the talent form.
- **Industry, press and partners:** people checking BOA's productions and credibility before booking talent, partnering or writing about the company.

## Product Purpose

BOA Network (Best Of All Networks) is a UK production company, founded by Joseph Boat and David O, that makes cinematic film, series and television and casts talent the industry overlooks. The site has to prove the work is cinematic and turn overlooked talent into applicants.

## Positioning

The productions are built around people with no clout, no money and no connections. Access is the point, not a side scheme, and it holds the same standard as everyone else.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: home, about, productions, talent roster and profiles, access (pathways, casting calls, FAQs), contact (general and talent forms).
- Forms submit through `src/lib/submitForm.js`, with a mailto fallback.
- Primary domain: https://www.boanetworks.com. Emails use @boanetworks.com.

## Brand Commitments

- The official logo: "BOA" in heavy block letters with a city skyline inside the O, "NETWORK" beneath, and a thin rule under that. Source file: `public/og-image.png`.
- Voice: direct, warm, no jargon, no dashes in sentence copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Mind States UK (series, crime/drama/thriller, 2026): Episode 1 and the official trailer on YouTube. A trailer frame is approved as the production still.
- Never fabricate: the talent roster names, founder bios and casting calls are placeholders until replaced. Add no invented credits, press, awards or partners.

## Product Principles

1. Prove the work is cinematic before asking anything of the visitor.
2. Every path leads to being seen: the talent form is the primary action.
3. Respect over hype: clear terms, straight answers.
